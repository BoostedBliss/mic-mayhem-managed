## 2024-10-31 - Web Audio GC Churn in requestAnimationFrame Loop\n**Learning:** The `audioEngine.ts` was re-allocating new `Uint8Array` instances inside its `getMetrics()` method, which is called on every `requestAnimationFrame`. This pattern produces extreme garbage collection pressure (creating 120 new typed arrays every second) that leads to micro-stutters during high-load audio operations.\n**Action:** Always pre-allocate and reuse typed arrays (e.g., `cachedTimeDomain`, `cachedFreqData`) when working inside high-frequency real-time update loops like Web Audio `getByteTimeDomainData`.
